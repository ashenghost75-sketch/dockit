Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Data Collection & Storage: DockIt uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Firestore to securely store your scanned receipts, warranty dates, and loyalty cards in an isolated per-user cloud vault.

Camera & Location: Camera access is used only when scanning receipts or barcodes. Location access is used to alert you when you are near a store with an active warranty or voucher.

Advertising & Subscriptions: Free-tier users may see ads served by Google AdMob. Subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete individual documents inside the DockIt app at any time, or request complete account and cloud vault deletion by emailing ashenghost75@gmail.com.
